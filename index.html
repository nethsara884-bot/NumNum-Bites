Our Story & Purpose

Born at Wayamba University of Sri Lanka with a mission to replace sugary junk snacks with real, natural energy.

Founded in 2026 • Wayamba University of Sri Lanka

From University Trials to Sri Lanka’s Favourite Healthy Bite

Num-num bites was started in 2026 by a group of students in Wayamba University of Sri Lanka who loved snacking but wanted a healthier option. We noticed that most students, office workers and gym-goers were eating chips, biscuits and sugary drinks to get quick energy. But after a short time, they felt more tired and unhealthy. So, we decided to create a snack that is tasty, healthy and gives real energy.

After many trials with natural ingredients like kittul jaggery, peanuts, dates and oats, we created Num-num bites, a healthy snack that gives you long-lasting energy without added sugar or preservatives. Today, Num-num bites is made for everyone who wants to stay active, focused and healthy at university, at work, or at the gym.
